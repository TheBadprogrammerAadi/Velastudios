Client project · Thai takeaway, East Grinstead

Thai Twist

A Thai takeaway kitchen in a car park on London Road. Most customers order by phone, so the website has one job: make pre-ordering effortless.

Business
Thai takeaway
Scope
Three-page website
Status
Live at thaitwist.co.uk

Visit live site ↗

What the site does

  • Shows whether the kitchen is open right now, and when it next opens, the moment the page loads.
  • Puts the full menu online, numbered exactly like the paper leaflet, so regulars can order by number.
  • Makes calling or WhatsApping a pre-order one tap from every page.
  • A dedicated Find us page with a map, because the kitchen is in a car park and easy to miss.

Design choices

Type
Fraunces in bold italics for the headlines, warm and confident, with a touch of Thai script for authenticity.
Colour
Near-black with chilli red and a golden yellow taken from the brand, so food photos glow.
Layout
Phone-first, with the call button always in reach and the menu split into clear sections.
Detail
A rolling ticker repeats the essentials: where to find them, the phone number, and that everything is cooked fresh.
