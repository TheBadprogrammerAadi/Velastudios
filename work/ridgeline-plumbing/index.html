Ridgeline Plumbing

An emergency plumber whose customers are stressed, on a phone, and need to call within seconds.

Business
Trades
Scope
One-page website
Status
Concept project
View live site

What the site does

  • A "Call now" button stays on screen the whole time on phones.
  • A 24/7 emergency banner answers the first question before it is asked.
  • Services in plain words, plus a list of towns so customers know they are covered.
  • A quote request form for jobs that can wait until tomorrow.

Design choices

Type
Archivo, stretched wide and heavy — loud, solid and impossible to miss.
Colour
Deep navy with safety-sign yellow for anything you can tap.
Layout
High contrast and big tap targets, built for one-handed use in a hurry.
Motion
Quick and punchy — nothing that delays the phone number.
