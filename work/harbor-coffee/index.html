Harbor Coffee

A neighbourhood café that needs regulars and passers-by to find its hours, menu and front door — fast.

Business
Café
Scope
One-page website
Status
Concept project
View live site

What the site does

  • Opening hours and directions sit in the first two scrolls, because that is what most visitors want.
  • A short, readable menu so people know what they are walking into.
  • An order-ahead form that lets the morning rush skip the queue.
  • Warm, photo-led layout that sells the feeling of the place before anyone visits.

Design choices

Type
Young Serif for headings, Figtree for everything else — friendly and a little old-fashioned, like a chalkboard sign.
Colour
Cream, espresso brown and a terracotta accent pulled straight from the cups.
Shape
An arched hero photo and a rotating "Open today" stamp give it a hand-made, local feel.
Motion
Gentle fades and a slow-scrolling strip — calm, like a quiet morning.
